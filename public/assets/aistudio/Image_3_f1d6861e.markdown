## Brand & Style
This design system expresses a kawaii-modern sensibility tailored for a contemporary fibre arts atelier and maker collective. It bridges soft, tactile warmth with polished digital elegance. The interface avoids juvenile tropes in favor of playful craft sophistication: pillowy surfaces, soft light-diffused radiance, and woven tactile metaphors.

The emotional arc is restorative, inspiring, and delightfully tactile. Micro-interactions should feel bouncy yet gentle, evoking the spring of spun wool, the slide of a shuttle across warp threads, and the soft pop of snap-fasteners. The visual style merges modern minimalism with playful rounded geometries (bubbly pill contours, soft marshmallow cards) and faint dotted/knitted textural accents that honor tactile textile crafts.

## Colors
The palette balances sweet confectionery pastels against grounding artisanal deep tones:

- **Primary (`#e8b4c8`)**: Spun Blossom Rose. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and soft focal halos.
- **Secondary (`#2d1f3d`)**: Deep Mulberry Plum. Provides authoritative structural contrast, typography hierarchy, sharp icons, and anchors navigational states.
- **Tertiary (`#d8b4e2`)**: Soft Heather Lilac. Used for secondary tags, craft badges, and layered container backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#faf6f0`)**: Cozy Carded Cream. Serves as the canvas foundation, lending a warm, non-clinical parchment background.

### Functional Accent Colors
- **Warm Butter (`#fef3c7`)**: Highlighting tips, pattern notes, and celebration states.
- **Minty Sage (`#d1fae5`)**: Gauge success markers, yarn stock status, and eco/material badges.
- **Surface Tiers**: Canvas begins at Warm Cream (`#faf6f0`). Cards sit on Pure Spun White (`#ffffff`) with subtle Rose/Mulberry tinted borders (`#f2e7eb`).

## Typography
The typography pairing establishes a tension between quirky editorial craft warmth and streamlined digital clarity:

- **Display & Headings (Epilogue)**: High-character, geometric, and softly expressive. Generates a cozy, artisanal studio feel with generous counters and curvaceous terminals.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Friendly, warm, and highly legible with rounded apertures that echo the kawaii ethos while retaining crisp reading rhythm.
- **Labels, Meters & Gauge Specs (Space Grotesk)**: Adds a distinct, modern maker-space technicality to yarn weights, hook sizes, loom warp counts, and pattern specs.

## Layout & Spacing
This system relies on a fluid 12-column responsive layout configured for visual breathability and showcase-driven imagery.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` gutters, `3rem` canvas margin with maximum content constraints at `1280px`.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, `1.5rem` margins. Side-by-side feed cards reflow to dual-column ribbons.
- **Mobile (below 640px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1rem` margins. Interactive cards stretch edge-to-edge within margins, with horizontal swiping pill reels for tags and yarn palettes.

Spacing tokens provide airy padding around bubbly components to avoid visual crowding. Studio canvas sections should feature an optional subtle knitted dot mesh (`radial-gradient(#2d1f3d10 1px, transparent 1px)` spaced at `16px x 16px`).
